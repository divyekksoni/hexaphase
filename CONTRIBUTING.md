# Contributing to Hexaphase

Thank you for helping improve Hexaphase. The framework is maintained by its creator, **Divyek Soni**, who makes the final decisions on changes to the core guide. This keeps the framework consistent, the way the Scrum Guide is kept by its authors.

## Contributions we especially want
- **Case studies.** You built something with Hexaphase: what worked, what didn't, and with which AI tools. Open an issue labelled `case-study`.
- **Example projects** that follow the template.
- **Translations** of `README.md` and `GUIDE.md` into other languages.
- **Tool integrations** (pointer files or settings for more AI tools).
- **Clarity fixes** to wording, typos and examples.

## Proposing a change to the framework itself
Open an issue labelled `proposal` that describes:
1. the problem you hit in a real project
2. the change you suggest
3. whether it breaks existing Hexaphase projects

Accepted changes are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Licensing of contributions
By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under the same terms as the file you change (CC BY 4.0 for documentation, MIT for `template/`), and that the work remains attributed as "Hexaphase SDLC Framework by Divyek Soni".
